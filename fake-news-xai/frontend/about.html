About TruthLens

TruthLens

AI-powered fake news detection for a more informed world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help users identify misinformation and make informed decisions before sharing content online.

How It Works

TruthLens analyzes text, audio, and video using AI models to identify suspicious patterns and credibility signals.

What We Check

Source credibility, manipulated language, supporting evidence, inconsistencies, and misinformation patterns.

Why TruthLens?

In today's digital world, information spreads faster than ever. Unfortunately, misinformation spreads just as quickly.

TruthLens aims to give users an easy way to analyze suspicious news and understand why content may be real, fake, or uncertain.
